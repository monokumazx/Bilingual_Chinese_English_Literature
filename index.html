📚 All Terms (所有)
Components (成分) 主语 · 谓语 · 宾语 · 定语 · 状语 · 补语
主语zhǔyǔsubject
Subject
执行动作或被描述的人或事物。通常在句首。
The person or thing performing the action or being described. Usually at the start.
小明在踢足球。（“小明”是主语）
Xiao Ming is playing soccer. ("Xiao Ming" is the subject.)
谓语wèiyǔpredicate
Predicate
句子中说明主语做什么或是什么的部分（通常是动词）。
The part of the sentence that tells what the subject does or is (usually the verb).
鸟儿飞。（“飞”是谓语）
Birds fly. ("Fly" is the predicate.)
宾语bīnyǔobject
Object
动作的承受者。通常跟在动词后面。
The receiver of the action. It usually follows the verb.
我喝水。（“水”是宾语）
I drink water. ("Water" is the object.)
定语dìngyǔattribute
Attribute / Modifier
修饰主语或宾语的词，常带“的”。
Words modifying the subject or object, often marked by “的” (de).
红色的苹果。（“红色的”是定语，修饰苹果）
Red apple. ("Red" is the attribute modifying apple.)
状语zhuàngyǔadverbial
Adverbial
修饰动词/谓语（时间、地点、方式），常带“地”。
Modifies the verb/predicate (time, place, manner), often marked by “地” (de).
他飞快地跑了。（“飞快地”是状语）
He ran quickly. ("Quickly" is the adverbial.)
补语bǔyǔcomplement
Complement
跟在动词后补充说明结果、程度或趋向，常带“得”。
Follows the verb to explain the result, degree, or direction, often marked by “得” (de).
由于紧张，他写得很慢。（“很慢”是补语）
Because of nervousness, he wrote very slowly. ("Very slowly" is the complement.)
Sentence Types (句类) 简单句 · 并列句 · 复合句 · 把字句 · 被字句 · 条件句 · 让步句 · 目的句 · 转折句
简单句jiǎndānjùsimple
Simple Sentence
仅有一个主谓结构的句子。
A sentence with only one subject-predicate structure.
他写了一篇文章。
He wrote an article.
并列句bìnglièjùcompound
Compound Sentence
由两个或多个意义相关的简单句用连词连接而成。
Two or more related simple sentences connected by coordinating conjunctions; meanings are parallel.
我喜欢篮球，也喜欢足球。
I like basketball and also like football.
复合句 / 复句fùhéjùcomplex
Complex Sentence
包含两个或多个分句，分句之间常有主从关系。
Contains two or more clauses, often with a main-subordinate relationship.
因为下雨了，我们只好待在家里。
Because it was raining, we had to stay at home.
把字句bǎ zì jùba-sentence
Ba-Sentence
强调对宾语的处理或处置的主动句。结构：主语 + 把 + 宾语 + 动词。
Active voice sentence focusing on the disposal of an object. Structure: Sub + 把 + Obj + Verb.
请把门关上。
Please close the door. (Lit: Please take the door and close it.)
被字句bèi zì jùbei-sentence
Bei-Sentence
被动句。结构：承受者 + 被 + 施事者 + 动词。
Passive voice sentence. Structure: Receiver + 被 + Agent + Verb.
蛋糕被弟弟吃了。
The cake was eaten by little brother.
条件句tiáojiànjùconditional
Conditional Sentence
表示条件与结果关系的复合句，常用“如果…就…”。
Expresses a condition-result relationship, often using "If... then..." (如果…就…).
如果天气好，我们就去郊游。
If the weather is good, we will go for an outing.
让步句ràngbù jùconcessive
Concessive Sentence
表示让步转折，通常使用“虽然…但是…”。
Expresses concession, usually using "Although... still..." (虽然…但是…).
虽然下雨了，我们仍然准时到达了学校。
Although it was raining, we still arrived at school on time.
目的句mùdìjùpurposive
Purposive Clause
表示目的的复合句，常由“为了”引导。
A complex sentence expressing purpose, often guided by "in order to" (为了).
他学习汉语是为了能读懂中文书籍。
He studies Chinese in order to read Chinese books.
转折句zhuǎnzhé jùpivotal
Pivotal / Transition Sentence
一种兼语式复句，前一个分句的宾语兼作后一个分句的主语，常表转折或关联。
A sentence structure realizing a turn or connection, often where the object of the first clause becomes the subject of the second.
如果你让我去，我就去。
If you let me go, I will go. (Shift from condition to result)
Punctuation (标点) 标点符号
标点符号biāodiǎn fúhàopunctuation
Punctuation
用于辅助文字表达语气、停顿的符号。
Symbols used to indicate tone, pause, and structure in writing.
，。！？；：等。
, . ! ? ; : etc.
句号jùhàoperiod
Period
表示陈述句结束。
Indicates the end of a declarative sentence.
逗号dòuhàocomma
Comma
表示句内停顿。
Indicates a pause within a sentence.
问号wènhàoquestion mark
Question Mark
表示疑问句结束。
Indicates the end of an interrogative sentence.
感叹号gǎntànhàoexclamation
Exclamation Mark
表示感叹句或强烈感情。
Indicates an exclamatory sentence or strong emotion.
Nouns/Verbs/Adj (实词) 名词 · 动词 · 形容词 · 量词
名词míngcínoun
Noun
表示人、地点、事物或概念的词。
A word that represents a person, place, thing, or idea.
“桌子”、“北京”、“幸福”都是名词。
"Table", "Beijing", and "Happiness" are all nouns.
动词dòngcíverb
Verb
描述动作、状态或事件的词。
A word describing an action, state, or occurrence.
请找出这句中的动词：“跑”、“吃”、“是”。
Please find the verbs in this sentence: "run", "eat", "is".
形容词xíngróngcíadjective
Adjective
用于描述或修饰名词或代词的词。
A word used to describe or modify a noun or pronoun.
她穿了一件漂亮的裙子。（“漂亮”是形容词）
She wore a beautiful dress. ("Beautiful" is the adjective.)
量词liàngcímeasure word
Measure Word / Classifier
表示名词单位的词（如：张、本、个）。
Words used to indicate the unit of a noun (e.g., piece, pair, cup).
一头牛，三本书，一张纸。
One cow, three books, one sheet of paper.
Pronouns (代词) 人称 · 指示 · 疑问
代词dàicípronoun
Pronoun
代替名词的词（如：我、你、它、这个）。
A word that substitutes for a noun (e.g., I, you, it, this).
那是我的书。（“那”和“我”是代词）
That is my book. ("That" and "my" are pronouns.)
人称代词rénchēng dàicípersonal
Personal Pronoun
我、你、他、她、它、我们、你们、他们等。
I, you, he, she, it, we, you (pl.), they, etc.
指示代词zhǐshì dàicídemonstrative
Demonstrative Pronoun
这、那、这些、那些等。
This, that, these, those, etc.
疑问代词yíwèn dàicíinterrogative
Interrogative Pronoun
谁、什么、哪、怎么等。
Who, what, which, how, etc.
Prepositions (介词) 时间 · 地点 · 方向 · 对象
介词jiècípreposition
Preposition
放在名词/代词前，表示时间、地点、方向或对象的词。
Words placed before nouns/pronouns to indicate time, place, direction, or object.
他在家里看书。（“在”是介词，表示地点）
He reads at home. ("Zai" is the preposition indicating location.)
从cóngfrom
From
表示起点。
Indicates starting point.
我从北京来。
I come from Beijing.
对duìto/towards
To / Towards
表示对象。
Indicates target or object.
他对我说。
He said to me.
Conjunctions (连词) 连接词 · 短语 · 分句
连接词 / 连词liáncíconjunction
Conjunction / Linker
用于连接词语、短语或句子的词。
Words connecting words, phrases, or sentences (And, Or, But, Because).
他们去了图书馆，因为要借书。
They went to the library because they needed to borrow books.
和héand
And
表示并列关系。
Indicates coordination.
我和你。
You and I.
但是dànshìbut
But
表示转折关系。
Indicates contrast.
我想去，但是没时间。
I want to go, but I don't have time.
因为yīnwèibecause
Because
表示因果关系。
Indicates cause.
Particles (助词) 结构助词 · 动态助词 · 语气助词
助词zhùcíparticle
Particle
虚词，表示语法关系或语气，单独使用没有具体意义。
Function words that indicate grammatical relationships or tone. They have no concrete meaning alone.
“的”、“了”、“吗”都是助词。
"De", "le", and "ma" are all particles.
的deattribute
Particle (Attribute)
连接修饰语和名词（领属/定语）。结构：修饰语 + 的 + 名词。
Connects descriptions to nouns (Possessive/Attributive). Structure: Description + 的 + Noun.
这是我最喜欢的书。
This is my favorite book.
地deadverbial
Particle (Adverbial)
连接修饰语和动词（状语）。结构：修饰语 + 地 + 动词。
Connects descriptions to verbs (Adverbial). Structure: Description + 地 + Verb.
他高兴地笑了。
He smiled happily.
得decomplement
Particle (Complement)
连接动词和结果/程度。结构：动词 + 得 + 修饰语。
Connects verbs to results/degrees. Structure: Verb + 得 + Description.
他跑得很快。
He runs very fast.
了leperfective
Particle (Perfective)
表示动作完成或状态改变。
Indicates a completed action or a change of state.
雨停了，我们可以走了。
The rain has stopped; we can go now.
吗maquestion
Question Particle
表示疑问语气。
Indicates a question.
你好吗？
How are you?
Others (其他虚词) 副词 · 叹词 · 拟声词 · 助动词
副词fùcíadverb
Adverb
修饰动词、形容词或其它副词，表示程度、频率、范围等。
Modifies verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs, indicating degree, frequency, scope, etc.
很、非常、都、也、不。
叹词tàncíinterjection
Interjection
表示感叹或呼唤。
Expresses exclamation or calling.
啊、哦、唉。
拟声词nǐshēngcíonomatopoeia
Onomatopoeia
模拟声音的词。
Words that imitate sounds.
汪汪、喵喵、哗哗。
助动词zhùdòngcíauxiliary verb
Auxiliary Verb
帮助主要动词表达可能、意愿等。
Helps main verb express possibility, willingness, etc.
能、会、可以、应该。
Patterns (核心句式) 把字句 · 被字句 · 存在句 · 连动句 · 兼语句
把字句bǎ zì jùba-sentence
Ba-Sentence
强调对宾语的处理或处置。结构：主语 + 把 + 宾语 + 动词。
Focuses on the disposal of an object. Structure: Sub + 把 + Obj + Verb.
请把门关上。
Please close the door.
被字句bèi zì jùbei-sentence
Bei-Sentence
被动句。结构：承受者 + 被 + 施事者 + 动词。
Passive voice. Structure: Receiver + 被 + Agent + Verb.
蛋糕被弟弟吃了。
The cake was eaten by little brother.
存在句cúnzàijùexistential
Existential Sentence
表示某处存在某人或某物。常用“有”或“是”。
Indicates existence. Often uses 有 or 是.
桌子上有一本书。
There is a book on the table.
连动句liándòngjùserial verb
Serial Verb Sentence
两个或以上动词连用，表示连续动作。
Two or more verbs used together for sequential actions.
他去商店买东西。
He went to the store to buy something.
兼语句jiānyǔjùpivotal
Pivotal Sentence
前一个动词的宾语兼作后一个动词的主语。
Object of first verb is also subject of second.
老师让我回答问题。
The teacher asked me to answer the question.
Clauses (从句) 定语从句 · 状语从句 · 名词性从句
定语从句dìngyǔ cóngjùrelative clause
Relative Clause
修饰名词的从句，常带“的”。
Clause that modifies a noun, often marked by 的.
我认识的那个人是医生。
The person I know is a doctor.
状语从句zhuàngyǔ cóngjùadverbial clause
Adverbial Clause
修饰主语的从句，表示时间、条件、原因等。
Clause modifying the main verb, indicating time, condition, cause, etc.
如果明天下雨，我们就不去。
If it rains tomorrow, we won't go.
名词性从句míngcíxìng cóngjùnominal clause
Nominal Clause
起名词作用的从句。
Clause that functions as a noun.
我知道他来了。
I know that he came.
Writing Lexicon (词汇) 写作 · 文学 · 修辞
写作xiězuòwriting
Writing / Composition
指写文章、作品的活动或过程。
Refers to the activity or process of writing articles or works.
他喜欢写作，常常把自己的经历和感想记录下来。
He likes writing; he often records his experiences and thoughts.
文学wénxuéliterature
Literature
以语言文字为媒介的艺术形式，包括小说、诗歌、散文等。
Art forms using language as a medium, including novels, poetry, prose, etc.
中国文学有着悠久的历史与丰厚的底蕴。
Chinese literature has a long history and rich heritage.
作者 / 作家zuòzhě / zuòjiāauthor/writer
Author / Writer
写作并创作作品的人。“作家”通常指职业或著名作者。
The person who writes. "Zuòjiā" usually implies a professional or famous writer.
这部小说的作者是著名作家鲁迅。
The author of this novel is the famous writer Lu Xun.
文稿wéngǎomanuscript
Manuscript / Draft
指尚未正式出版或印刷的文字稿件。
Refers to text manuscripts or scripts that have not yet been officially published or printed.
报告的文稿已经写完，下一步进行修改。
The manuscript of the report is finished, the next step is revision.
手稿shǒugǎohandwritten
Handwritten Manuscript
作家或作者亲笔写下的原稿。
Original manuscripts written by the author by hand.
博物馆里收藏着这位诗人的亲笔手稿。
The museum houses this poet's autograph manuscript.
草稿cǎogǎorough draft
Rough Draft
写作初步完成但未经修改的草稿。
Preliminary drafts or outlines that have not been revised.
在写正式文章之前，他先做了很多草稿。
Before writing the final article, he first made many drafts.
文章wénzhāngarticle
Article / Composition
指成文的文章，一般由标题、正文、段落等组成。
A written work consisting of a title, body text, and paragraphs.
这篇文章的水平很高。
This article is of a very high standard.
段落duànluòparagraph
Paragraph
文章中围绕一个主题而组织在一起的一段文字。
A section of text organized around a theme. Usually starts on a new line.
每个段落都应该有一个清晰的主题句。
Each paragraph should have a clear topic sentence.
句子jùzisentence
Sentence
由词构成、表达完整意思的语言单位。
A language unit composed of words expressing a complete idea.
这句话语法正确，表达清楚。
This sentence is grammatically correct and expresses clearly.
Structure & Logic (逻辑) 主题句 · 过渡词 · 连接词 · 过渡句
主题句zhǔtíjùtopic sentence
Topic Sentence
段落或文章开头的一句话，用来概括主要观点。
A sentence at the start of a paragraph summarizing the main point.
这篇说明文的主题句放在了第一段。
The topic sentence of this expository essay is placed in the first paragraph.
过渡词guòdùcítransition words
Transition Words
用于连接句子或段落，使文章连贯的词语。
Words used to connect sentences or paragraphs for coherence (e.g., However, Therefore).
然而，虽然遇到了困难，他还是坚持完成了任务。
However, although he encountered difficulties, he still persisted in completing the task.
连接词 / 连词liáncíconjunction
Conjunction / Linker
用于连接词语、短语或句子的词。
Words connecting words, phrases, or sentences (And, Or, But, Because).
他们去了图书馆，因为要借书。
They went to the library because they needed to borrow books.
承上启下chéngshàng qǐxiàtransition
Transitional (Connecting Previous and Next)
承接上文，开启下文。
Connecting previous and next.
开门见山kāimén jiànshānstraight to point
Getting Straight to the Point
文章一开始就直入主题。
Getting straight to the point.
